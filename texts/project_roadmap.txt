                    JOB SEARCH TRACKER - PROJECT ROADMAP
================================================================================

PROJECT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A professional portfolio piece demonstrating full-stack web development and 
data science skills. The application enables job seekers to track applications,
visualize their job search progress, and analyze patterns in their search.
